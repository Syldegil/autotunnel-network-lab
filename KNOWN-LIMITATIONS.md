# Limitations

USB/hotspot forwarding, IPv6/DNS leakage prevention, VPN transitions, Doze, reboot and endurance remain unqualified. A Linux namespace model is not Android netd/BPF/hardware offload. Route drafts do not become enforced rules merely because they render correctly. Public package identity and signing differ from the private app; privileged clients must be separately provisioned and qualified. No personal signing key or privileged daemon deployment is supplied.

The app can initiate user-selected diagnostic DNS/TCP/TLS probes. An app screen or PASS from a simulated backend is not proof of an active anti-leak barrier. No blanket safety, router completion or production recommendation is made.
